<a href="https://artsandrec-op.org/arboretum/terra-luna/">Terra Luna</a> is an annual event by <a href="https://www.quixoticfusion.com/">Quixotic</a> that transforms the Overland Park, Kansas Arboretum into a magical forest full of musicians, performers as well as light and art installations.

For the last several years, I've had the opportunity to do this projection piece at a lovely pond near the entrance of the experience.

<div class="video-embed"><iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/kZe4X7cDLSM" title="Quixotic - Terra Luna 2023" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

Each year I created fun new visuals in TouchDesigner and mapped them to the trees and landscaping on the bank of this pond. We have done both two and three-projector setups for this space. Outdoor projection on irregular surfaces is a really fun and unique challenge.

<div class="video-embed"><iframe src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/742397883?h=7b25786f73" frameborder="0" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allow="autoplay; fullscreen; picture-in-picture; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; web-share"   allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

If you're in KC in September, I highly recommend you come out and see the amazing work done by this fantastic crew.
