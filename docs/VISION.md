# the816.com Vision

## Purpose
A portfolio site that demonstrates depth of experience in interactive installations and creative technology through behind-the-curtain thinking. Not a catalog of work, but a curated showcase that reveals conceptual frameworks and technical problem-solving.

## Primary Audience
- **Potential collaborators** in experiential design, interactive installations, and creative technology
- **Potential employers** seeking senior creative technologists with leadership experience
- **Peers in the field** - other practitioners working in the "fuzzy space between art and technology"

## Tone & Philosophy
- **Thoughtful** - Demonstrates depth without being verbose
- **Technically deep** - Shows real expertise and problem-solving capability
- **Not pretentious** - Approachable, human, genuine enthusiasm for the work
- **Experimental but professional** - Signals creative thinking while maintaining credibility

## Content Approach
This is a **fresh start** for online presence:
- Curated project highlights (max 10) rather than comprehensive archive
- Writing that spans technical tutorials, project narratives, and industry thinking
- Behind-the-curtain insights that establish expertise depth
- Teaching materials available as resources, but not primary focus

## What Success Looks Like
Visitors should leave thinking:
- "This person operates at the intersection of art and technology in a sophisticated way"
- "They have deep technical capability AND conceptual thinking"
- "I'd like to work with/hire/collaborate with them"
- "This site itself demonstrates their design sensibility"
